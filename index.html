Information about cats

Cats are among the oldest pets. They value their independence, but can become very attached to their owner.

  • They sleep up to 16 hours a day
  • Purring has a calming effect
  • They see very well in the dark

Why do people love cats?

Cats create a cozy atmosphere, help people relax, and are excellent at sensing people's moods. They don't require constant attention, but they're always there when needed most.
